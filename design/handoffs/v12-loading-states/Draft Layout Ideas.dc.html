PICKING BETWEEN THEM
1a is the least work to build and the easiest to explain, but champion select is timed and it hides half the context on every tap. 1b keeps both on screen and has room for bans and comp reads, at the cost of a busier top third. 1c feels best in the hand and scales to long reasoning, but the sheet has to be dragged with a thumb during a 30-second timer.
