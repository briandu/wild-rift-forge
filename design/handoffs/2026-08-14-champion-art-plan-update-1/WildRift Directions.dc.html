Three directions, one brief
Homepage + counter results for each · desktop 1240px · static mockups
Each direction was built as if a separate team took the brief: different layout, navigation, density, type and champion-art treatment. All three share the same dark, Discord-adjacent base so the comparison is about structure and personality, not brightness.
1A
Competitive Analytics
A ranked-climb instrument. Icon rail plus persistent filter bar, graphite surfaces, one amber accent, and counter results laid out as a ranked table with scores, win rates and difficulty. Champion art appears only as small square portraits — data carries the page. Different from B and C: highest density, most numbers, least art.
